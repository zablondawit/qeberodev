## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit a website.  
They help websites function properly, remember preferences, and provide analytics data.

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

### 3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for core website functionality (e.g., page navigation, security, form handling).  
Without these, some services may not work properly.

### 3.2 Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use the Website so we can improve performance and user experience.  
Examples may include:

- Page views
- Traffic sources
- Session duration
- Device/browser types

### 3.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies remember choices you make (e.g., language or region preferences) to improve personalization.

### 3.4 Marketing/Newsletter-Related Technologies

If used, these help us understand engagement with emails/newsletters (e.g., open/click behavior), subject to applicable legal requirements and consent where required.

## 4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use trusted third-party services that set cookies on our behalf, such as:

- Analytics providers
- Embedded content providers
- Email/newsletter tools
- Security/anti-spam services

These providers have their own privacy/cookie practices. Please review their policies separately.

## 5. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

- Keep the Website secure and functional
- Measure and improve website performance
- Understand visitor behavior and content effectiveness
- Support newsletter and communication performance tracking (where applicable)
